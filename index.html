Find your device tier

Shaders behave very differently depending on what you're running.

📱

Low-end (1–2GB)

Stick to the "Low-end" filter — these packs skip expensive shadow and reflection passes.

📲

Mid-range (3–4GB)

Most of the "Realistic" and "Vibrant" packs are tuned for this tier.

🖥️

Flagship / high-end

"RTX-style" packs add reflections and bounce lighting — expect a real FPS cost.
